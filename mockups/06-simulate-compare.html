Why Google Cloud is slower here

Writes to Checkouts wait for the Spanner leader in us-central1. European users pay the transatlantic round trip on two writes per checkout, and PaymentService runs only in us-central1.

Why AWS stops at 3,900/s

Lambda Managed Instances reach the account's 1,000 concurrent executions in us-east-1 first. The compute itself has room; the limit is a quota you can ask to raise.
